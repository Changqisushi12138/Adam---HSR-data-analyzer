# Adam---HSR-data-analyzer
一个爱玩游戏的人为了提升游玩体验写的小工具，作用为帮助游戏理解不深的大众玩家更好的分析自己的账号该如何调整以达到账号水平的提升。A person who loves playing 'Honkai: Star Rail' wrote a small tool to enhance the gaming experience. Its purpose is to help general players who do not have a deep understanding of the game better analyze how to adjust their accounts in order to improve their account level.
